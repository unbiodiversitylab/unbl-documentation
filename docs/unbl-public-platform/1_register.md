Before you begin exploring the maps, register for the UN Biodiversity Lab.

1. Click the 'Launch map' button on the [UN Biodiversity Lab](https://unbiodiversitylab.org/) website.

	![UNBL website home page with 'Launch map' highlighted in the top menu](images/register-01.png)

2. Once this has loaded, select the account icon in the top right-hand corner and choose 'sign in'. In the loaded page then choose 'sign up'. Enter your email, set your password, name, country, institution/organization, and organization type to sign up.

	![Map app with the account icon highlighted in the top right corner](images/register-02.png)

	![Sign-up form with the 'Sign Up' tab highlighted above the registration fields](images/register-03.png)

3. You will receive an email within a few minutes. Follow the instructions in this email to verify your account. 

	![Verify Your Email message with LOGOUT and SEND EMAIL VERIFICATION LINK buttons](images/register-04.png)

4. Once your account is verified, you can log in using your email address and password each time you access the platform.

	![Sign In form with email and password fields and SIGN IN button](images/register-05.png)

5. You can log out at any time by clicking on your user icon and selecting 'Sign Out'.

	![Map app with the user icon highlighted in the top right corner](images/register-06.png)
	![Account menu with 'sign out' highlighted below the user name](images/register-07.png)
